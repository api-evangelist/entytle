Service revenue growth in industrial manufacturing refers to the systematic expansion of aftermarket income – parts sales, maintenance contracts, field service, repairs, upgrades, and lifecycle support – generated from an OEM’s existing installed base of equipment. It is the single most effective lever for improving margins, stabilizing cash flows, and increasing enterprise value. How effective?
